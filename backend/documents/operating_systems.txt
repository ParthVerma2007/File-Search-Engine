Operating systems manage computer hardware resources and provide services for application software.
Process management handles process states, context switching, inter-process communication, and multithreading.
CPU scheduling algorithms include First-Come-First-Served, Shortest Job First, Round Robin, and Priority Scheduling.
Memory management coordinates virtual memory, paging, segmentation, and page replacement policies like LRU.
Deadlock conditions require mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption, and circular wait.
